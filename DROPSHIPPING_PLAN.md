# Dropshipping Startup Plan (Australia-focused)

A step-by-step plan to go from zero to first sales in about 30 days, with a chosen product, the numbers behind it, and what to do week by week.

> Prices and fees below are approximate for 2026. Check current pricing before you commit money.

---

## 0. The honest reality check

- Dropshipping is a **marketing business**. You don't hold stock, but you do pay for ads, handle refunds, and answer customers.
- Most stores fail because of **slow shipping, thin margins, or no ad-testing budget**. The plan below is built around avoiding those three problems.
- Budget to start properly: **AUD $1,000–$1,500** (store, samples, and about $600–$900 for ad testing). You *can* start with about $300 using only organic TikTok, but it will be slower.
- Expect your first 1–3 products to flop. That's normal: you're paying to learn what sells.

---

## 1. The product: a "Dog Road-Trip" store

### Chosen niche: car travel accessories for dog owners

| Hero product | Dog car seat hammock / back-seat cover (waterproof, with mesh window) |
|---|---|
| Add-ons (upsells) | Dog seatbelt tether, collapsible travel water bottle/bowl, car-seat pet booster, lint/hair roller |
| Target customer | Australian dog owners aged 25–55 who drive to beaches, parks and on weekend trips |
| Price to customer | Hammock AUD $69–$89, bundle AUD $109–$129 |
| Your cost (product + shipping) | About AUD $20–$32 for the hammock from an AU-warehouse supplier |

### Why this product passes the test

Use this checklist for **any** product you consider. A product should hit at least 7 of the 9:

1. ✅ **Solves a real problem**: dog hair, mud and scratches in the car, plus dogs jumping into the front seat.
2. ✅ **Emotional buyer**: pet owners spend freely on their dogs (about 40% of Aussie households have a dog).
3. ✅ **Can sell for 3x cost or more**, which leaves room for ad spend.
4. ✅ **Not easily found in local shops** at the same quality and price (Kmart has basic versions; you win on quality, bundle and branding).
5. ✅ **Demonstrates well on video**: a muddy dog jumps in and the seat stays clean. That's a perfect TikTok/Reels hook.
6. ✅ **Light and not fragile**, so it's cheap to ship and has few damage claims.
7. ✅ **Upsell potential**: bundles raise your average order value.
8. ✅ **Evergreen**: people travel with dogs all year, with peaks in summer and school holidays.
9. ✅ **No certification or safety risk**: avoid electronics, kids' products, cosmetics and supplements when starting.

### Backup product ideas, if the tests fail

- **Karting / motorsport garage gear**: digital tyre pressure gauges, tyre warmers, pit trolleys and helmet bags. Small niche, but passionate buyers, and you already know the space.
- **Car detailing kits**: cordless mini vacuums, detailing brushes and interior cleaning bundles.
- **Home/kitchen organisation**: drawer systems, fridge organisers and pantry label kits.

## 2. Validate before you spend (Days 1–3)

1. **TikTok search**: search "dog car seat cover" and "dog car hammock". Look for videos posted in the last 60 days with 100k+ views. Recent viral content means live demand.
2. **Meta Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library): search the same terms with country set to Australia. Ads that have run for **30+ days** are profitable, because nobody keeps a losing ad running. Save the good ones for creative inspiration.
3. **Google Trends**: set the region to Australia over 5 years. You want a stable or rising trend, not a one-off spike.
4. **Amazon.com.au / eBay**: read the **1–3 star reviews** of competitors. Those complaints (slipping, not waterproof, bad fit) become your selling points and your product selection criteria.
5. **Price check**: note what competitors charge. Position yourself in the middle or upper-middle, not the cheapest.

**Go/no-go:** if you find active ads 30+ days old, recent viral videos, and a supplier cost of 1/3 of the selling price or less, go ahead.

---

## 3. Business setup (Days 1–5)

1. **Get an ABN**: free at abr.gov.au. Start as a sole trader, which is simplest; talk to an accountant about a company later.
2. **Business name**: register it with ASIC (about $45/yr) if you trade under a name other than your own. Check that the .com.au domain and Instagram/TikTok handles are free first.
   - Name ideas: *PawTrip*, *Muddy Paws Co*, *Backseat Buddy*, *RoadDog Supply*.
3. **GST**: you only have to register once turnover reaches **$75k/yr**. Track it from day one.
4. **Separate bank account**: use a separate account for all business income and costs, for example Up, ING or your bank's business account.
5. **Bookkeeping**: use a spreadsheet at first, then Xero or QuickBooks once you're making sales.
6. **Know Australian Consumer Law**: you must offer refunds or replacements for faulty or not-as-described items. You're the seller, not your supplier. Budget about 3–5% for refunds.

## 4. Find a supplier (Days 3–7)

**Rule #1: ship from inside Australia.** 3–7 day delivery converts far better, and gets far fewer "where's my order?" emails, than 15–25 days from China.

| Supplier option | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Dropshipzone** (AU) | AU warehouses, fast local delivery, Shopify integration. Best starting point. |
| **AU warehouse on CJdropshipping** | Filter by "AU warehouse". Wide range and low prices. |
| **AliExpress (AU-warehouse / "Choice" items)** | OK for testing, but check the shipping time carefully. |
| **Local wholesalers** | Search "pet accessories wholesale Australia". Later you can buy small bulk and use a 3PL for better margins. |

**Supplier checklist:**
- Order **samples** of your top 2–3 hammocks (budget about $100). Test the fit in your own car, check it's waterproof, and **film your own content with the samples**.
- Check that tracking numbers sync automatically to your store.
- Ask about their return and defect policy, and whether they can do plain packaging with no China invoices inside.
- Keep a **backup supplier** listed for your hero product.

## 5. Build the store (Days 5–12)

### Platform
**Shopify** (Basic plan, about AUD $40–60/month, with a free trial). Install:
- The supplier app (Dropshipzone or CJ) to import products and auto-fulfil orders
- A reviews app (Judge.me, which has a free tier)
- A bundle/upsell app (for example "Frequently bought together")
- Klaviyo or Shopify Email for email marketing

### Store structure
- **Home page**: hero video of the dog and hammock, one clear headline ("Keep your car clean. Take your dog everywhere."), trust badges, reviews.
- **Product page** (this matters most):
  - 6–8 photos plus a short video (use your own sample footage and lifestyle shots)
  - Benefit-led bullet points: waterproof, scratch-proof, universal fit, installs in 60 seconds, machine washable
  - A size/fit guide ("fits most sedans, SUVs and utes")
  - Shipping info: "Ships from Australia, 3–7 business days"
  - Bundle offer: "Road-Trip Kit: hammock + seatbelt + water bottle, save 20%"
  - FAQ and a 30-day money-back guarantee
- **Required pages**: Contact, Shipping Policy, Refund Policy, Privacy Policy, Terms. Shopify has free policy generators.
- **Payments**: Shopify Payments, PayPal, Afterpay (popular in Australia) and Apple/Google Pay.
- **Free shipping** over $79, built into your pricing.

### Branding (keep it cheap)
- Logo from Canva (free)
- 2 brand colours and 1 font
- A friendly, Aussie, outdoorsy tone of voice

## 6. Pricing and the numbers

Example for a single hammock order:

| Line | AUD |
|---|---|
| Selling price | $79.00 |
| Product + AU shipping (supplier) | −$28.00 |
| Payment fees (about 2.5%) | −$2.00 |
| Refund/chargeback allowance (5%) | −$4.00 |
| **Gross profit before ads** | **$45.00** |
| Break-even ad cost per purchase (CPA) | **$45** |
| Target CPA | $20–30 (that leaves about $15–25 profit per order) |

**Bundles are where the profit is.** A $119 bundle costing about $42 leaves about $70 before ads.

Formulas to keep in a spreadsheet:
- `Break-even ROAS = Selling price ÷ Gross profit before ads`. Here that's 79 ÷ 45 ≈ 1.75, so every $1 of ads must bring in at least $1.75 in sales.
- `Net profit = Revenue − product costs − fees − ad spend − apps/subscriptions`

## 7. Content and marketing (Days 10–30)

### A. Organic first (free): TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
Make **2–3 short videos a day** for the first 2 weeks. Hook formats that work:
1. "POV: your dog after the beach vs your car seats" (a before/after mess)
2. "Things I wish I bought sooner as a dog owner"
3. "Installing this in 30 seconds" (a satisfying install video)
4. "Replying to @user: will it fit a Hilux?"
5. Borrow friends' dogs. Cute dogs are free engagement.

Link the store in your bio. Use TikTok Shop if it's available to you, since it can convert directly.

### B. Paid ads (once you have 3–5 good videos)
- **Platform**: Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads, with TikTok Ads second.
- **Test structure**: 1 campaign (Sales objective), broad targeting across Australia, ages 25–60, with 3–5 different video creatives. Budget **$30–50/day**.
- **Rules**:
  - Run each test for 3–4 days before judging it.
  - Kill any ad that spends 1.5× your break-even CPA (about $65) with no sale.
  - Duplicate winners and raise the budget 20–30% every 2–3 days.
  - Always keep testing new creatives. The creative is what makes ads work, far more than targeting.
- Install the **Meta Pixel and Conversions API** (via the Shopify Facebook channel) *before* you spend a dollar.

### C. Email (free money)
Set up 3 automated flows in Klaviyo or Shopify Email:
1. **Abandoned cart**: sent at 1 hour, 24 hours (with a 10% code) and 48 hours
2. **Welcome series**: 10% off the first order for signing up
3. **Post-purchase**: install tips, a review request at day 10, and a cross-sell at day 20

### D. Micro-influencers
Send free products to 10–20 dog accounts on Instagram/TikTok with 5k–50k followers. Ask for honest content you can reuse in your ads (get written permission).

## 8. Operations and customer service

- **Order flow**: a customer orders, the app sends the order to the supplier, the supplier ships, and tracking syncs to the customer automatically.
- Check orders **every day**: stock levels, stuck orders and supplier errors.
- Answer emails within **24 hours**. Use saved replies for "where's my order", returns and sizing.
- Set up a tracking page (a free app such as "Track123") to cut down "where's my order?" emails.
- Keep a simple log of each issue, what caused it and how it was fixed.

## 9. 30-day action checklist

**Week 1: Research and setup**
- [ ] Validate the niche with TikTok, the Meta Ad Library, Google Trends and Amazon reviews
- [ ] Register an ABN, and a business name if needed
- [ ] Open a separate bank account
- [ ] Sign up to Dropshipzone and/or CJ, then shortlist 3 hammocks and 3 add-ons
- [ ] Order samples

**Week 2: Build**
- [ ] Start the Shopify trial, then buy the domain and set up the theme
- [ ] Build the product pages, bundle, policies and payments (including Afterpay)
- [ ] Install the Pixel, reviews, upsell and email apps
- [ ] Set up TikTok and Instagram accounts and post your first 5 videos

**Week 3: Content and soft launch**
- [ ] Film 15–20 videos with the samples and dogs
- [ ] Post 2–3 a day organically
- [ ] Set up the 3 email flows
- [ ] Place a test order yourself to check the whole flow end to end
- [ ] Contact 10–20 micro-influencers

**Week 4: Paid testing**
- [ ] Launch the Meta test campaign at $30–50/day with 3–5 creatives
- [ ] Review the numbers every day against your break-even CPA
- [ ] Kill losing ads and scale winners
- [ ] If there are still no sales after about $500–700 of spend with good creatives, go back to Step 1 with a backup product

---

## 10. Months 2–6: scaling and building a brand

1. **Scale** winning ads slowly, and add TikTok Ads and Google Shopping.
2. **Expand the catalogue** within the niche: dog travel beds, boot liners, cooling mats and car ramps for older dogs.
3. **Improve margins**: once you're selling 10+ a day, ask your supplier for a better price, or buy 100–300 units and store them with an AU 3PL.
4. **Private label**: add custom branding and packaging. It means a better product, better reviews and a real brand you could sell later.
5. **Hire help**: a virtual assistant for customer service at about 20 orders a day.
6. **Register for GST** when you get close to $75k turnover. See an accountant at this point.

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

- ❌ Shipping from China with 3-week delivery
- ❌ Picking products because *you* like them, without checking the data
- ❌ Stealing other stores' videos and images. Make your own content.
- ❌ Giving up after $100 of ad spend, or spending $2,000 on one losing product
- ❌ Ignoring customer emails, which leads to chargebacks and frozen payment accounts
- ❌ Selling electronics, cosmetics or kids' products before you understand the compliance rules
- ❌ Mixing personal and business money
